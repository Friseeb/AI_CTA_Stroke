# Open-source landscape: mitral valve / annulus / MAC on CT (searched 2026-10-08)

Short answer: **yes, two things now exist that did not a year ago** — (1) an openly licensed nnU-Net v2 that emits a `Valve_Mitral` label on CT/CECT (STOPSTORM, UMC Utrecht, Zenodo 13752275), and (2) the first public **labelled cardiac-CT mitral-annulus dataset** (MICCAI 2026 MVAA challenge, 27 labelled + 1 040 unlabelled CTs, CC BY-NC) with three open-source top-team training recipes. Nothing open does MAC quantification directly; the only validated MAC-on-non-gated-CT tool (Bunkerhill MAC) is closed. Everything already covered (PlatiPy, TotalSegmentator tasks, VISTA-3D, MM-WHS, MOOSE, TAVRP-PL) is unchanged, with one update: a user opened TotalSegmentator issue #607 (2026) offering to contribute a mitral-valve model trained on MVAA data — no maintainer reply yet.

## Best options for this group

**1. STOPSTORM Network B as a drop-in mitral ROI + HU-threshold MAC (effort: 1–2 days).** Download model 603, run via nnU-Net v2 (or the rtpipeline wrapper), take `Valve_Mitral`, dilate by ~5–8 mm, subtract TotalSegmentator `coronary_arteries`/`aortic_sinuses` and chamber lumen, threshold ≥130 HU (adjusted for contrast — use a per-case blood-pool-based threshold, e.g. LA mean + 2–3 SD, since the annulus abuts opacified LA/LV), report volume/Agatston-like score. Caveats: trained on non-gated CT (good match) but only 21 CECT cases and VT patients; DSC ~0.4 means position is right but boundaries aren't — fine for MAC-in-ROI, wrong for annulus diameters. CC BY 4.0, no commercial restriction.

**2. MVAA CT data + top-team recipe → own annulus nnU-Net, then fine-tune on ~30 of your CTAs (effort: 2–4 weeks).** Download the 27 labelled + 1 040 unlabelled MVAA CTs, train nnU-Net v2 ResEnc-M per MVAA-UniSeg/mvaa2026 (CT DSC ~85 reported), pseudo-label the unlabelled set, then annotate 20–40 of your own non-gated head-neck-chest CTAs (nnInteractive or MVAA-model-proposals edited in Slicer) to close the domain gap (gating, FOV, 0.5 mm slices). Output is a true annulus ring → annulus area/diameters plus a principled MAC shell. Constraint: CC BY-NC on data propagates to models; fine for academic stroke research, not for a product.

**3. Geometric annulus from your existing TotalSegmentator `heartchambers_highres` (effort: 3–5 days, zero new models).** Extract the LA∩LV contact surface (dilate each 1–2 voxels, intersect), fit a plane + closed curve (the PlatiPy approach), build a toroidal shell around the curve and quantify calcium there; optionally register the Rodero/Strocchi mesh `Mitral valve plane` as a prior to stabilise cases where the atrioventricular groove is poorly separated. Robust, licence-free, immediately explainable, but biased wherever MAC itself distorts the chamber masks (MAC is bright and often absorbed into the LV myocardium/LA wall by TotalSegmentator) — so validate against route 1 on the same cases.

Recommended sequencing: do 3 and 1 this month in parallel (they cross-validate), and start annotations for 2 if annulus geometry (not just MAC burden) becomes an endpoint. Atlas registration alone (MM-WHS/Rodero) is not worth pursuing as a primary route given non-gated motion and the 0.4–0.45 DSC ceiling the 2026 comparison shows even for trained models.
